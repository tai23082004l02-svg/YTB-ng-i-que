# Production notes

- Original English script: 20 chapters, 3,603 whitespace-delimited words.
- Narration: local Kokoro-82M, American English, am_michael. TTS speed 1.04; final mild tempo adjustment 1.028788 applied equally to audio and subtitle timecodes.
- Final runtime: approximately 24 minutes 45 seconds.
- 288 new source illustrations generated in 24 storyboard sheets; 268 distinct source cells selected for the edit. No source cell is reused in the final shot list.
- Fixed full-screen illustrations with straight cuts every approximately 5.2–5.7 seconds. No zoom, pan, camera shake, top title bar, chapter counter, or permanent black subtitle strip.
- English subtitles: model token timing, clipped correctly across shot boundaries, burned into the picture; standalone SRT included.
- Format: 1280 × 720, 24 fps, H.264/AAC. Audio target: -16 LUFS, -1.5 dBTP, 48 kHz mono.
- Final audio uses measured two-pass loudness normalization.
- Normalization leaves -2.0 dBTP headroom before AAC encoding so the encoded deliverable stays below the -1.5 dBTP ceiling.
- Thumbnail uses separate newly generated artwork and typography.
- No third-party music. Nothing is published to YouTube.

The source artwork is stylized and partly speculative, especially ancient faces, clothing, and personal encounters. Scientific illustrations are conceptual, not measurements or laboratory results. See SOURCES.md for evidence limits. An initially generated misleading ladder-like panel was excluded in favor of a DNA laboratory illustration (shot 235, zero-indexed).
