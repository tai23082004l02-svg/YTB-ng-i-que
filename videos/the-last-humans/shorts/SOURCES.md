# Sources and evidence limits — The Last Humans: 12 Shorts

Original English scripts adapted from the channel's long documentary. References rechecked 2026-10-24. The Shorts are independent explanations, not verbatim excerpts. Images are artistic reconstructions and qualitative concepts, not recovered portraits, measured phylogenies or original laboratory results. This is not a systematic review.

## References consulted for this batch

1. **Smithsonian Human Origins, Homo sapiens.** https://humanorigins.si.edu/evidence/human-fossils/species/homo-sapiens — modern species context. All living human populations are Homo sapiens. Shorts01,10,12.
2. **Smithsonian Human Origins, Frequently Asked Questions.** https://humanorigins.si.edu/education/frequently-asked-questions — branching evolution and shared ancestry with other primates. Humans did not descend from living chimpanzees. Short02.
3. **Smithsonian Human Origins, Homo neanderthalensis.** https://humanorigins.si.edu/evidence/human-fossils/species/homo-neanderthalensis — anatomy, fire, tools, variable subsistence and approximate disappearance around40,000 years ago. Neither brow shape nor brain volume establishes an IQ ranking. Shorts01,03,09,12.
4. **Reich et al. (2010), Genetic history of an archaic hominin group from Denisova Cave in Siberia.** https://www.nature.com/articles/nature09710 — public abstract consulted for finger-bone DNA, Denisovan identification and wider Asian/genetic context. No2010 admixture percentage is generalized to every living person or adopted as a current universal estimate. Short04.
5. **Slon et al. (2018), The genome of the offspring of a Neanderthal mother and a Denisovan father.** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-018-0455-x ; open-access version https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6130845/ — public abstract and targeted sections of the open full text retrieved through Europe PMC. Confirms first-generation parentage and female sex from chromosome coverage. The family’s appearance, circumstances, language and private relationship are unknown. Short05. A generic reconstruction is not a forensic likeness.
6. **Smithsonian Human Origins, Homo floresiensis.** https://humanorigins.si.edu/evidence/human-fossils/species/homo-floresiensis — estimated female adult height106 cm/3ft6in; fossil evidence about100,000–60,000 years and a different tool range. The Short says about3½feet for one adult, not3 feet for every individual. Origins remain debated; last fossil is not exact extinction. Short06.
7. **Smithsonian Human Origins, Homo erectus.** https://humanorigins.si.edu/evidence/human-fossils/species/homo-erectus — approximate1.89million–110,000year fossil range under the page's grouping. Short07 conservatively says well over a million years and explicitly recognizes classification differences. Present absence is not proof of historical incompetence.
8. **Skov et al. (2022), Genetic insights into the social organization of Neanderthals.** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-05283-y — open abstract/main text consulted for close relatives and small-community evidence at Chagyrskaya, within a study sampling two Siberian sites. Not a census of every Neanderthal and not proof of one complete extinction cause. Short08; context for09.
9. **Smithsonian Human Origins, Ancient DNA and Neanderthals.** https://humanorigins.si.edu/evidence/genetics/ancient-dna-and-neanderthals — background on methods, introgression, Denisovans and ancestry estimates. Short10 uses approximate1–2% for many people with ancestry outside Africa, qualified by population/method, not all people identically.
10. **Chen et al. (2020), Identifying and Interpreting Apparent Neanderthal Ancestry in African Individuals.** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2020.01.012 — public abstract consulted through Semantic Scholar with matching title/authors/DOI. Supports stronger-than-previously-recognized ancestry signal and migrations back to Africa, alongside earlier population history. No full publisher-text review claimed. Short10 avoids a clean African/non-African binary, personality inference, racial hierarchy or species labels for modern people.
11. **Huerta-Sánchez et al. (2014), Altitude adaptation in Tibetans caused by introgression of Denisovan-like DNA.** https://www.nature.com/articles/nature13408 — public abstract consulted: a region around EPAS1, Denisovan-related introgression and selection in a low-oxygen-availability environment. Short11 does not claim one gene explains all physiology, that an individual can safely ignore altitude illness, or that all donor history is known. No diagram purports to show measured physiology or a precise oxygen molecular structure.
12. **Smithsonian Human Origins, Humans Change the World.** https://humanorigins.si.edu/human-characteristics/humans-change-world — consulted only for the broad agricultural transition within roughly the past12,000 years and regional differences. Its older embedded archaic-human dates and bottleneck examples are not used. Species pages above supply the fossil context. Short12 distinguishes the Neanderthal disappearance from much later farming; no universal date of farming's first appearance is asserted.

## Interpretation across the Shorts

- Species, populations, communities and archaeological cultures are different categories. No final exact count of prehistoric tribes is supplied.
- Relatives did not all live in one place, share one time range, meet one another or vanish through one demonstrated mechanism. The50,000-year opening is not a full-cast reunion.
- Reconnecting branches describe possible/established introgression where supported; the images do **not** establish Flores ancestry in modern humans or specific unverified parentage among named fossil lineages.
- First-generation Denisova11 parentage differs from population-level ancestry accumulated over many generations. Modern ancestry is not a claim of a modern person's literal archaic parents.
- Neanderthal tools, fire and food-use evidence reject simplistic incompetence; they do not recover every social practice or quantify intelligence.
- Population connections, demography, environment, competition and interbreeding are possible interacting explanations. No universal extermination campaign or guaranteed single survival advantage.
- Faces, clothing, camps, expressions and group interactions are invented respectful visual explanations. Particularly for Denisovans, confident art does not imply a confidently known appearance. Small Flores figures are drawn as mature adults with facial lines, not children.
- The short titles and spoken qualifiers are designed together: estimated height, varied ancestry, incomplete evidence and regional differences remain in the actual narration.

## Art provenance and curation

Parent source art already belongs to this project. Four parent sheets were viewed and used as style/character references: `../project/assets/sheet-05.png`, `sheet-08.png`, `sheet-09.png`, and `sheet-17.png` (paths here refer to the long film's project). Portrait illustrations were recomposed for9:16, with new scene layouts, rather than claiming a center crop preserves every person in a landscape group. The parent MP4 and its voice track were not changed.

The Shorts have their own19 sheets, each4×4 portrait cells:304 source panels,252 selected. Sixteen ambiguous panels were excluded before timing/rendering; see `project/curation-notes.json`. Rejected examples could imply unsupported Flores introgression, literal multi-parent descent, a bone-derived forensic face, incorrect molecular bonds, or misleading era/animal associations. They remain in source for provenance, not permission to reintroduce them. **Sheet indices are directory-specific: do not confuse parent and Shorts asset manifests.** The parent's excluded ladder panel is not used as literal footage.
