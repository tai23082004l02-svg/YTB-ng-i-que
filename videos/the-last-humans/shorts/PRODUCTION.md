The whole caption text is checked against narration.

Each voice track uses its own measured two-pass normalization,192 kHz oversampled peak limiting, compensated latency, measured post-limiter gain and single-generation128k AAC from original WAV. Final levels are measured again in the actual MP4; targets around−16LUFS, true peak≤−1.5dBTP. Settings and attempts are saved per episode and must not become a preset for unrelated narration.

Sixteen source panels were excluded before selecting shots for unsupported or ambiguous lineage/introgression relationships and other scientific visual problems. Source18 provides neutral alternatives. Final shots/curation manifests are authoritative; do not simply select every original panel again. All diagrams are conceptual, not original data or an exact measured phylogeny. See SOURCES.md.

Per-Short checks: whole-file decode; H.264/AAC streams and9:16 dimensions; duration45–70 seconds; timing continuity and class-specific scene lengths; caption order/bounds/full text; absence of excluded panels; final integrated loudness/true peak; cover dimensions; title/description limits; file sizes and hashes. Covers and representative interior frames were reviewed in addition to source boards. This is not a claim of exhaustive human viewing/listening. Package ZIP CRC, relative links and copied hashes are checked on delivery.

Each cover is a frame extracted from its actual video, not a separate misleading scene. Titles, descriptions, tags, pinned comments, SRT, alternative titles and an ordering CSV are supplied. Related video selection and publication remain with the channel owner; no URL or schedule was invented and nothing was posted to YouTube.
