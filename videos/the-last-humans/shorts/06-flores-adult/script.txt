This ancient human was an adult, not a child.
One individual of Homo floresiensis, discovered on the Indonesian island of Flores, is estimated to have stood about three and a half feet tall.
These small-bodied humans had a distinctive combination of anatomical traits. They were not simply modern people waiting to grow taller.
Their evolutionary origins remain debated. Island environments can reshape populations, but that does not settle the exact route that produced them.
Stone tools also warn us against treating a small body or brain as a shortcut for judging practical ability.
Their ending remains uncertain. The youngest fossil discovered is not automatically the last individual who lived.
Flores gives us a remarkable human relative, and questions about its history. It does not give us a solved case with an identified killer.
