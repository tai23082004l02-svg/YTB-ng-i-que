The Milky Way has billions of stars, countless planets, and an enormous head start. So why haven't we confirmed another technological civilization?

This illustrated documentary explores the Fermi paradox: what we have actually searched, what a null result means, and why life, intelligence, technology, and detectable signals are different questions.

From the Drake equation and the cosmic haystack to interstellar expansion, the Great Filter, and possible technosignatures, we separate observations from assumptions. The zoo hypothesis and dark forest are discussed as speculation, not established explanations. No alien discovery is being announced.
