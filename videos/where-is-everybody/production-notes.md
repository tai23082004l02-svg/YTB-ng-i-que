# Production notes — Where Is Everybody?

Original English script: 28 chapters, 5,028 whitespace-separated words. Kokoro am_michael, speed 1.07. Native narration length 2122.975 seconds with short chapter pauses. No additional tempo adjustment and no music. Final container 2122.975 seconds, rounded 35:23.

34 generated contact sheets of 12 panels each: 408 source illustrations, 377 distinct selected panels. Static crops and straight cuts every 5.17–5.83 seconds. Actual source-sheet separator positions are recorded in project/grid-crops.json; a 6-pixel inset removes borders. Scaling preserves aspect ratio and a fixed center crop fills the frame. H.264 1280 × 720 at 24 fps. Illustrations are scaled from panels in the source sheets; this is not native 4K art or full character animation. No zoom, pan, shake, top header or scene counter.

683 English subtitle cues from Kokoro token timing, burned into the picture with white outlined text, no permanent black strip. ASS style: DejaVu Sans bold, 29 px at 1280 × 720; 2 px outline, 1 px shadow, 65 px side margins and 28 px bottom margin. Matching SRT supplied. Measured two-pass loudness normalization; final AAC measured -16.73 LUFS integrated, -1.72 dBTP true peak, 2.20 LU loudness range. Mono 48 kHz AAC, nominal 128 kbps. Settings belong to this narration, not a universal preset.

The opening candidate signal and its later interference explanation are fictional examples. Nora, Eli and Dr Vega are storytelling avatars. Charts, star maps and systems are conceptual and not to scale; no plot is presented as actual detection data. Hypothetical collectors, artifacts and settlements are speculative diagrams or thought bubbles. No UFO evidence or alien bodies. Drake organizes unknowns; Great Filter and behavioral explanations are not established outcomes.

Curation: excluded source sheet-18/cell-9 because the hypothetical artifact resembled a human Mesoamerican temple. Shot 251 uses a natural mineral in sheet-30/cell-5 instead. The source is retained for provenance; do not reuse the excluded panel as a valid scene.

Validation includes full-file decode, stream/chapter checks, subtitle bounds and order, continuous shot timing, unique-panel count, audio measurement, ZIP CRC, representative source and final-film visual review. This does not claim exhaustive human listening. Thumbnail and upload metadata are prepared; the channel owner publishes on YouTube.
