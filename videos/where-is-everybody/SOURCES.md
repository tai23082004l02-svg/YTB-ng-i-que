# Sources and interpretation notes — Where Is Everybody?

This original illustrated documentary explores the Fermi paradox: the tension between some expectations about technological life and the absence of verified evidence for another technological civilization. **It does not report an alien detection or claim that the entire sky has been exhaustively searched.** Research pages and abstracts listed below were consulted during production on 24 October 2026. Older studies are described within their historical scope, not as the complete present-day search record.

## Planets, habitability and the observational baseline

- [NASA Science — Exoplanets](https://science.nasa.gov/exoplanets/): planets are widespread; discoveries use several methods; the catalog continues to grow. The narration deliberately says “thousands,” rather than freezing a changing count. The retrieved page reported more than 6,000 confirmed planets.
- [NASA Science — Are We Alone?](https://science.nasa.gov/universe/search-for-life/): the search is only beginning, many planets are known, major biological and technological probabilities remain unknown, and no evidence of life beyond Earth is established in this overview.
- [NASA Science — The Habitable Zone](https://science.nasa.gov/exoplanets/habitable-zone/): the orbital region where surface liquid water could be possible under appropriate conditions. Habitable-zone membership is not a finding of oceans, life, breathable air, or human habitability. Red-dwarf longevity and planetary habitability are distinct issues.

The Milky Way's approximate size and stellar population are broad order-of-magnitude background descriptions, not exact counts. Galaxy views, planetary systems, orbits, light paths and star sizes in the illustrations are conceptual and not to scale. Light-travel delays are not faster-than-light messaging; a ten-light-year one-way journey implies at least twenty years for a prompt question-and-reply exchange.

## Fermi and Drake

- [SETI Institute — The Fermi Paradox](https://www.seti.org/research/seti-101/fermi-paradox/): historical association with Fermi's 1950 lunchtime question; possible expansion arguments; caution about extrapolating from limited observations. The film does not impersonate Fermi or claim an exact historical reconstruction of the lunch.
- [SETI Institute — Drake Equation](https://www.seti.org/drake-equation-index): factors organize estimates of detectable civilizations, including the duration of detectable activity. The equation is a framework for assumptions, not a measured census or an established numerical answer. Detectable technological duration need not equal the lifespan of a species.

## Origin of life and inference from one example

David S. Spiegel and Edwin L. Turner, **Bayesian analysis of the astrobiological implications of life's early emergence on Earth**. [Open preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1107.3835). Abstract consulted. The inference from Earth's early life is sensitive to assumptions and observer selection. An independent origin would provide much stronger information than a single lineage. The film does not assign a universal abiogenesis rate or treat one model's prior choices as scientific consensus.

Life, complex life, intelligence, technological activity and detectable emissions are separate questions. Evolution is not portrayed as an inevitable ladder ending in humans. Ocean-world constraints are possibilities, not a proof that aquatic technology is impossible.

## Search coverage and null results

Jason T. Wright, Shubham Kanodia and Emily Lubar, **How Much SETI Has Been Done? Finding Needles in the n-Dimensional Cosmic Haystack** (2018). [Open abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.07252). Abstract consulted. A quantitative radio-search haystack has defined boundaries and dimensions. Its completeness fraction cannot stand for all possible searches or all technological activity. The narration deliberately avoids presenting one tiny fraction as a timeless universal percentage.

Danny C. Price and collaborators, **The Breakthrough Listen Search for Intelligent Life: Observations of 1327 Nearby Stars over 1.10–3.45 GHz** (2019). [Open abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.07750). Abstract consulted. This historical survey describes target counts, bands, drift-rate limits, sensitivity and rejection of interference. Its zero surviving candidates apply to that analysis and its assumptions, not to every transmitter or the whole universe. The film uses it as grounding for the explanation of survey design, not as a claim to summarize every later survey.

Earth's own radio visibility depends on emission type, power, direction, schedule, distance, receiver and processing. No universal detection radius is assigned. An expanding light front is not a uniformly readable shell of television broadcasts. The film's spectral spikes and time-frequency plots are conceptual drawings, not actual candidate data.

## Verification and broader technosignatures

- [SETI Institute — Protocols for an ETI Signal Detection](https://www.seti.org/research/seti-101/protocols-for-an-eti-signal-detection/): authentication, independent investigation, careful communication and preserving evidence. The page retrieved includes a declaration dated June 2026. The film discusses verification principles, not a claim that a discovery has occurred or that every candidate will be conclusively resolved.
- [NASA — NASA Is Taking a New Look at Searching for Life Beyond Earth](https://www.nasa.gov/feature/nasa-is-taking-a-new-look-at-searching-for-life-beyond-earth): a historical 2018 overview of radio, laser, large-structure and atmospheric technosignature possibilities. Its dated program descriptions are not represented as a complete statement of current NASA policy.

The opening observatory event and its later interference resolution are **explicitly invented teaching scenarios**, not a disguised account of a real detection. Independent checks do not automatically prove alien technology; natural and human explanations must be addressed. An unexplained candidate is not positive proof merely because it remains unexplained.

**NASA and the Search for Technosignatures: A Report from the NASA Technosignatures Workshop** (2018), [open report](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.08681). Abstract and targeted full-text sections consulted: “Limits and the Limitations of Limits” (printed p. 9), and section 2.5.1 on energy budgets, waste heat, collector swarms and natural dust backgrounds (printed pp. 16–17). These sections support the distinction between method-specific limits and universal absence, as well as the infrared search discussion. The report is historical, not a census of all searches through 2026.

Hypothetical energy collectors are shown as a sparse orbital swarm rather than an asserted rigid shell. Energy budgets and infrared emission motivate searches for certain designs; geometry, temperature, backgrounds and natural dust matter. No infrared excess or unusual dimming is labeled a discovered megastructure. Ordinary thermodynamic reasoning is not a guarantee that every possible advanced civilization is detectable.

## Interstellar expansion as a model

Jonathan Carroll-Nellenback, Adam Frank, Jason Wright and Caleb Scharf, **The Fermi Paradox and the Aurora Effect: Exo-civilization Settlement, Expansion and Steady States** (2019). [Open abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.04450). Abstract consulted. Models include finite probe speeds and ranges, stellar motion, settlement lifetimes and partially inhabited steady states. They demonstrate outcomes under assumptions, not inevitable behavior or an observed alien distribution.

Sub-light travel is physically possible in principle but practical journeys face energy, reliability, dust, radiation, navigation and, for humans, life-support constraints. Schematic travel networks are not engineering plans. The film does not claim that all civilizations expand forever, or that none ever choose expansion. Human history is not treated as a universal alien psychology.

## Great Filter and being early

- Robin Hanson, [**The Great Filter — Are We Almost Past It?**](https://mason.gmu.edu/~rhanson/greatfilter.html): the original argumentative framework about difficult transitions. Page consulted. This is a theoretical argument with strong assumptions, not a discovered law, measured alien extinction rate or countdown to human catastrophe. The documentary presents uncertainty and competing interpretations more cautiously than treating every premise as established.
- Abraham Loeb, Rafael A. Batista and David Sloan, **Relative Likelihood for Life as a Function of Cosmic Time** (2016). [Open abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.08448). Abstract consulted. Future-life conclusions are conditional on assumptions about low-mass-star habitability and the model. Stellar longevity alone does not establish that humanity is early.

The zoo hypothesis and “dark forest” are presented as speculative stories about motives, not observational findings. Unknown motives cannot be read directly from a null signal. Future risk discussions distinguish the end of a transmitting phase, institutional collapse and species extinction. No numerical survival probabilities are invented.

## Artifacts, image provenance and scientific boundaries

The connection to [Before Us](../before-us/SOURCES.md) is about detectability and preservation. It is not evidence for prehistoric visitors. Human monuments and documented human achievements are not attributed to aliens. No UAP image, alleged alien body, or fabricated excavated machine is used as evidence.

All visual assets are original AI-generated 2D illustrations. Nora, Eli and Dr Vega are fictional storytelling avatars. Hypothetical machines, habitats and imagined civilizations appear as conceptual diagrams or separate thought bubbles. Laboratory scenes are explanatory, not actual photographs of discoveries. Contact sheets contain source panels; the final selected panels and timing are recorded in `project/shots.json`. Any excluded or replaced panel is documented in `project/curation-notes.json`.

A guessed NASA technosignatures URL returned 404 and was not used as a source. Research downloads, source HTML and intermediate WAV files remain local; no full article text or paper PDF is republished in this repository.
