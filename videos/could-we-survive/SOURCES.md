# Sources and limits of the thought experiment

This original illustrated documentary combines evidence about ancient environmental crises with explicitly hypothetical modern scenarios. No industrial human civilization experienced the Big Five. The video does not claim scientifically established human survival percentages, a dated doomsday forecast, or a demonstrated technology that can protect billions of people from every ancient-scale event.

Sources were consulted during production on 24 October 2026. Several institutional websites returned access errors; accessible original papers, university material and the official IPBES archive were used instead. No quotation is attributed to text that could not be read.

## Mass-extinction definition and chronology

- Barnosky et al. (2011), **Has the Earth's sixth mass extinction already arrived?**, Nature. Abstract consulted for the roughly three-quarters-of-species benchmark, elevated modern extinction rates, and difficulties comparing fossil and modern records. This is a benchmark, not a universal stopwatch or a percentage of individual people.
  https://www.nature.com/articles/nature09678
- NASA, **Earth Facts**. Planetary age of approximately 4.5 billion years. The Big Five belong to the much more recent history of complex life rather than being evenly spaced through Earth's entire age.
  https://science.nasa.gov/earth/facts/
- UC Museum of Paleontology, **Ordovician**, **Devonian** and **Permian** period exhibits. Used for broad palaeoenvironment and life-form context. Older numerical timescales on teaching pages are not treated as definitive current boundary dates.
  https://ucmp.berkeley.edu/ordovician/ordovician.php
  https://ucmp.berkeley.edu/devonian/devonian.php
  https://ucmp.berkeley.edu/permian/permian.php

Narration dates are approximate. Devonian losses occurred across episodes; they are not one instantaneous event. Different dating studies and revisions can place particular boundaries or pulses at somewhat different ages. No exact date determines a universal modern outcome.

## Late Ordovician

**Tempo of the Late Ordovician mass extinction controlled by the rate of climate change** (2025), Science Advances, DOI 10.1126/sciadv.adv6788. Abstract and open-access text consulted through Europe PMC. The study examines two phases and the importance of the rate of climatic change. Its high-precision South China chronology motivates rounding the narration to roughly 443 million years ago; regional dated sequences do not remove all global correlations and uncertainties.
https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adv6788
https://europepmc.org/articles/PMC12124363

Modern cooling, migration and crop-response scenes are explanatory scenarios, not forecasts of current human-caused warming reversing into an Ordovician glaciation.

## Late Devonian

**Timing and pacing of the Late Devonian mass extinction event regulated by eccentricity and obliquity** (2017), Nature Communications, DOI 10.1038/s41467-017-02407-1. Open-access text consulted for episodic losses, reef impacts, chronology and uncertainty in causal chains.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-017-02407-1
https://europepmc.org/articles/PMC5741662

The script discusses interacting hypotheses rather than naming one settled cause for every pulse. Oxygen stress in seawater is distinguished from atmospheric oxygen loss; a local modern dead zone is not equated with the entire Devonian crisis. Modern fisheries and livelihood scenarios are conditional reasoning, not numerical output from this ancient study.

## End Permian and heat

**Temperature-dependent hypoxia explains biogeography and severity of end-Permian marine mass extinction** (2018), Science, DOI 10.1126/science.aat1327. Abstract consulted through Europe PMC's indexed record. Modeling and the fossil record connect ocean warming, oxygen loss and aerobic habitat limits; the paper is not a forecast of human mortality in a hypothetical modern replay.
https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aat1327


The UCMP Permian exhibit supports the extraordinary severity of the marine crisis and the distinction between major groups of land and marine organisms. The wider scientific interpretation associates Siberian Traps magmatism with greenhouse forcing and interacting marine/terrestrial stresses. The narration avoids a precise universal species-loss percentage, a single-cone-volcano picture, and claims that every possible modern population would necessarily die.

Vecellio et al. (2022 issue; published online December 2021), **Evaluating the 35°C wet-bulb temperature adaptability threshold for young, healthy subjects (PSU HEAT Project)**. Abstract and open-access text consulted. Experimental results do not support using a universal 35°C wet-bulb threshold as a safe/dead boundary. Age, health, activity and exposure conditions matter; the video gives no personal safe-exposure formula.
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8799385/
https://doi.org/10.1152/japplphysiol.00738.2021

**Declining oxygen in the global ocean and coastal waters** (2018), Science, DOI 10.1126/science.aam7240. Abstract consulted through Europe PMC. Supports the roles of warming, nutrient loading, microbial respiration, oxygen solubility and resupply, together with ecological and fishery consequences.
https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aam7240

## End Triassic: several stresses rather than uniform warming

- **Pulsed volcanic sulfur emissions linked to the end-Triassic terrestrial crisis** (2026), Science Advances, DOI 10.1126/sciadv.adz6570. Open-access text consulted. Geochemical and biomarker evidence supports investigation of sulfur deposition, plant loss and associated fire effects during CAMP volcanism; the mechanism is not reduced to a single generic warming story.
  https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adz6570
  https://europepmc.org/articles/PMC13170647
- **Metamorphic sulfur release as a driver of sustained cooling and mass extinction** (2026), Science Advances, DOI 10.1126/sciadv.aee2277. Abstract and open-access text consulted. Modeling suggests sulfur-driven cooling pulses can be superimposed on longer greenhouse warming, with implications for large igneous province crises. This is a modeled mechanism, not proof of one exact temperature history everywhere.
  https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aee2277
  https://europepmc.org/articles/PMC13344362

The narration was revised during production to include potentially cooling pulses and uncertainty about sequence and regional effects. Modern carbon-removal or sunlight-modification discussion is an assessment of limitations, not a deployment prescription or a demonstrated cure for a geological eruption province.

## Chicxulub, sunlight and planetary defense

- **Chicxulub impact winter sustained by fine silicate dust** (2023), Nature Geoscience. Abstract consulted. Simulations assess dust, sulfur and soot contributions and reductions in photosynthetically active radiation. The modeled duration and magnitude are scenario-dependent; no single exact darkness timer is presented as certain for every modern impact.
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s41561-023-01290-4
- NASA, **DART**. Demonstrated kinetic impact changed the motion of the small asteroid moonlet Dimorphos in 2022. This is not a test on a Chicxulub-scale object and does not demonstrate that any threat can be deflected with any warning time.
  https://science.nasa.gov/mission/dart/

Regional effects of impact are distinguished from global food-production effects. Tsunamis are not depicted in the narration as identical waves crossing every mountain; birds are identified as surviving dinosaurs. Deccan volcanism is acknowledged as part of the broader environmental context without removing the impact's central role.

## Food resilience and essential infrastructure

ALLFED, **Publications and reports** and organization research overview. Consulted for the existence of research on abrupt sunlight reduction, alternative/resilient foods, microbial protein, feedstocks, energy, deployment, infrastructure and food-system disruption. These are research proposals and modeling studies with assumptions, not evidence that ready-made systems could feed the entire world immediately.
https://research.allfed.info/research/publications-and-reports
https://allfed.info/

The documentary deliberately avoids presenting any proposed resilient food as a guaranteed global pantry. Calorie production, nutrition, safety, water, energy, industrial inputs, logistics and governance are separate requirements. The shelter, grid, migration and supply-chain examples are original dependency analyses rather than quantified forecasts. Nuclear power is neither depicted as inevitably exploding nor as infrastructure-free electricity.

## The sixth extinction

IPBES (2019), **Summary for policymakers of the global assessment report on biodiversity and ecosystem services**. Original report downloaded from the official Zenodo archive; archive-provided checksum verified; relevant text, including statement A5, consulted. The report estimates around one million species threatened with extinction, many within decades unless drivers are reduced. Threatened is not already extinct, and the estimate is not a guaranteed fixed deadline. Major direct drivers include changes in land and sea use, direct exploitation, climate change, pollution and invasive alien species.
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3553579
https://zenodo.org/records/3553579

The 2019 estimate is attributed as an estimate, not represented as a newly calculated 2026 census. The film distinguishes accelerated biodiversity loss and the discussion of a sixth extinction from having already demonstrated a full Big Five-level species-loss total. It also distinguishes human catastrophes from mass extinctions across unrelated branches of life.

## Interpretation of illustrations and outcomes

Ancient life, modern characters, facilities and catastrophe scenes are AI-generated artistic reconstructions. Diagrams are conceptual; they are not experimental measurements, official projections, precise migration maps or survival-score charts. Images show no real emergency unfolding now. Ancient ecosystems do not contain modern humans, and modern scenarios are identified in the narration as thought experiments.

Verdicts are conditional: warning time, speed, duration, geography, food, water, energy, public health and cooperation determine which options may remain. Individual survival, persistence of connected populations and continuity of the current industrial civilization are evaluated separately. No minimum number of humans, extinction probability or promise of universal rescue is invented.

An AI-generated Triassic marine panel containing living trilobites was excluded during review, since trilobites were already extinct by that period. The selected shot list uses a conceptual fossil-boundary illustration instead; see project/curation-notes.json.
