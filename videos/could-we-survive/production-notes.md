# Production notes

- Original English script: 28 chapters, 4949 whitespace-delimited words.
- Narration: local Kokoro-82M, American English, am_michael, TTS speed 1.07. Additional tempo factor 1.041171498 applied to audio and all timecodes; final conversational pace approximately 143 words per minute. No silence padding was added to inflate runtime.
- Runtime: 34 minutes 30 seconds. Source narration before the final tempo adjustment: 2155.225 seconds.
- 32 new source sheets, 384 illustrations before selection; 371 unique source cells used.
- Fixed full-screen stills, straight cuts every 5.25–5.79 seconds. No zoom, pan, shake, title bar, scene counter or permanent black caption strip.
- Subtitles: 681 English cues, model token timing adjusted with audio; clipped at frame-aligned cut boundaries. Standalone SRT included.
- One generated Triassic living-sea illustration contained trilobites; excluded and replaced with fossil-boundary evidence. See project/curation-notes.json.
- Picture: H.264, 1280 × 720, 24 fps. Source panels are smaller than the final frame and are upscaled; these are not native 4K illustrations.
- Sound: measured two-pass normalization, AAC mono 48 kHz at 128 kbps. Pre-encode true-peak ceiling -2.5 dBTP; measured encoded loudness -16.76 LUFS and true peak -1.70 dBTP.
- Separate new thumbnail artwork and typography, plus a ready-to-copy YouTube upload kit. No third-party music.
- Ancient ecosystems are artistic reconstructions. Modern disaster scenes are hypothetical, and diagrams are conceptual rather than official forecasts or measured survival probabilities.
- No YouTube upload or scheduling was performed by the assistant.
