TITLE
Were We Earth's First Civilization?

DESCRIPTION
If an industrial civilization existed millions of years before humans, what traces could survive—and would we recognize them?

The Silurian Hypothesis is a scientific question about detectability, not an announcement that an ancient technological civilization has been discovered. This illustrated documentary follows the evidence from abandoned cities and sediment cores to carbon isotopes, synthetic materials, natural reactors, and geological false positives.

We ask what might remain of us after deep time, why a strange chemical signal does not automatically prove industry, and what a convincing investigation would actually need. We also examine the PETM comparison and explain why preservation limits do not make every imagined history equally likely.
