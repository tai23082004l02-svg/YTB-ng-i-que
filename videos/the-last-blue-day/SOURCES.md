# Sources and scientific limits — The Last Blue Day

Original English illustrated documentary for **The Long Wonder**. References accessed 2026-10-24. This is a mechanism-based explanation, not a dated forecast for a particular family, continent or last organism. Future scenes and interventions are hypothetical. No copyrighted papers are redistributed.

## Main references actually consulted

1. **NASA Space Place, “How Old Is the Sun?”** https://spaceplace.nasa.gov/sun-age/en/ — readable article consulted. Broad ~5-billion-year red giant scale. This is not the date of maximum expansion or the end of surface habitability.
2. **NASA Science, Sun: Facts.** https://science.nasa.gov/sun/facts/ — fusion, stellar structure and giant/white dwarf future. The page also compresses future stages in its public-facing lifetime summary; the film distinguishes them using the Space Place account and the stellar model below.
3. **K.-P. Schröder and R. C. Smith (2008), “Distant future of the Sun and Earth revisited.”** https://arxiv.org/abs/0801.4031 ; https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2008.13022.x — abstract and downloaded PDF consulted, with targeted full-text review of current luminosity increase, solar evolutionary stages, mass loss, orbit and tides. Reports ~10% luminosity increase over the next billion years and engulfment near tip-RGB ~7.59 billion years from now in that calculation. Not a universal exact fate/date; mass loss alone does not ensure orbital escape. Do not confuse stellar age with time from now.
4. **NASA Earth Observatory, “The Carbon Cycle.”** https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/the-carbon-cycle/ — slow weathering/rock/volcanism and fast biological cycle sections consulted. Geological feedback is not a fast solution to modern emissions. Simplified arrows omit many reservoirs and processes.
5. **R. J. Graham, I. Halevy and D. Abbot (2024), “Substantial extension of the lifetime of the terrestrial biosphere.”** https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.10714 ; https://arxiv.org/html/2409.10714v1 — abstract and HTML full text consulted, particularly methods, results, climate discussion and caveats. Certain weathering assumptions allow land-plant persistence ~1.6–1.86 Gyr and thermal rather than CO2-starvation termination. Global-mean models do not resolve all refuges, clouds, geography, evolution or nutrient constraints. These ranges are model outcomes, not a newly fixed end-of-life date. C3/C4 spoken as “C three/C four” for narration.
6. **K. Ozaki and C. T. Reinhard (2021), “The future lifespan of Earth’s oxygenated atmosphere.”** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41561-021-00693-5 — public abstract consulted, not claimed as full subscription-paper review. Mean future oxygen-rich lifetime 1.08 ± 0.14 Gyr (1σ), with threshold **O2 >1% of present atmospheric level**, not 1% oxygen by volume and not a human breathing threshold. In that model deoxygenation precedes extensive surface-water loss. It must not be stitched together with the different 2024 plant model as one internally consistent dated simulation.
7. **Penn State (2022), “Humans can’t endure temperatures and humidities as high as previously thought.”** https://www.psu.edu/news/research/story/humans-cant-endure-temperatures-and-humidities-high-previously-thought — readable research summary consulted, describing PSU H.E.A.T. experiments. Supports rejecting a universal safe/fatal wet-bulb number. Film gives no threshold guaranteeing human survival or a date of human extinction.
8. **NASA Science, Venus: Facts.** https://science.nasa.gov/venus/facts/ — dense hot atmosphere and planetary differences. Venus illustrates divergent rocky-planet climate, not a verified exact reconstruction of Earth's future composition, pressure or water history.
9. **NASA Science, Causes of Climate Change.** https://science.nasa.gov/climate-change/causes/ — present warming attribution and solar irradiance sections consulted. Current rapid warming is primarily human-driven; billion-year solar evolution is a separate issue.
10. **NASA, Environmental Control and Life Support Systems (ECLSS).** https://www.nasa.gov/reference/environmental-control-and-life-support-systems-eclss/ — present air/water support research and equipment are not demonstrated self-sufficient geological-time civilizations. No percent “perfect closure” or centuries-safe reproduction claim is made.
11. **D. G. Korycansky, G. Laughlin and F. C. Adams (2001), “Astronomical engineering: a strategy for modifying planetary orbits.”** https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0102126 — abstract consulted. A proposed sequence of gravitational encounters is speculative engineering, not tested Earth relocation. Film deliberately omits proposed encounter mass/cadence and does not certify safety, Moon retention or long-term control.

12. **R. Angel (2006), “Feasibility of cooling the Earth with a cloud of small spacecraft near the inner Lagrange point (L1).”** https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0608163103 — abstract retrieved via Crossref metadata and consulted. Describes a proposed distributed deflecting sunshade. The paper addresses a proposed climate intervention, not a demonstrated billion-year solar-evolution defense. Film does not endorse its cost, deployment time, mass or lifetime estimates as established capability.

## Core distinctions and calculations

- Outdoor human habitability, land plants, oxygen-rich atmosphere, surface oceans, microbial refuges and survival of the rocky planet are **different endpoints**.
- Incoming flux scales as luminosity / distance². For a **10% luminosity increase**, equal flux needs distance ratio sqrt(1.1) = 1.04880885, approximately **5% farther**. This is only flux geometry, not a climate simulation or executable orbital-rescue plan.
- A moist greenhouse allows a wetter upper atmosphere and eventual water loss. A runaway greenhouse concerns radiative limits and severe heating. No claim that the sea drains, boils instantly, or is lost at one universally agreed date.
- Oxygen fraction ≠ partial pressure. One-hundredth present oxygen ≠ atmosphere with 1% oxygen by volume ≠ human-safe air.
- A habitable-zone detection is not proof of breathable air, life or a landing site. Migration, sample preservation and maintaining an entire biosphere are not equivalent.
- The Sun is not expected to undergo a core-collapse supernova. Giant-stage imagery must not appear as a near-term climate event.
- No identified exact last refuge, future country safety map, human extinction date, universal wet-bulb cutoff, guaranteed habitat or operational planetary rescue system is claimed.
- The last-morning scene, anonymous future inhabitants, archive, shades and relocation are illustrative thought experiments. Mara is a modern presenter, not an immortal witness of geological epochs. Modern model-discussion scenes do not depict actual researchers' likenesses.
- Values in narration are rounded and qualified. Uncertainty ranges from different studies are not a pooled confidence interval or one synchronized timeline.

## Production and access scope

Independent prose and original AI-generated illustrations; conceptual diagrams, not copied research figures or measured data plots. Public NASA/academic pages and open-access full texts above were used to check mechanisms and major numerical signposts. Paywalled material was not bypassed. A guessed arXiv ID returned an unrelated UV-spectroscopy paper; it was rejected and is **not** evidence for the film. A PMC shade landing page returned a browser-verification stub; that stub is not treated as a read scientific paper. The Angel abstract was subsequently accessible via Crossref; only that abstract, not the browser-verification stub or full paper, was used.
