The Sun does not need to explode to end our world. It only needs to keep shining.

How much longer can Earth remain a home? Follow the science of a slowly brightening Sun, the rock–carbon cycle, the future of plants and oxygen, and the long-term loss of oceans. The end of human outdoor habitability, the decline of forests, the last microbes, and the possible destruction of the planet are different questions.

We also examine hypothetical responses: sealed habitats, planetary sunshades, moving Earth's orbit, and migration. These are possibilities to investigate—not proven rescue plans.

There is no single scientifically agreed date for Earth's “last day.” Estimates depend on what is being measured and on model assumptions. The 2021 oxygen model and 2024 plant study are compared as separate investigations, not stitched into one fixed countdown.
