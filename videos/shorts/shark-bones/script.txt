If a shark ate you, would it poop out your bones? First of all... terrible vacation. But don't picture a tiny skeleton coming out the other end.

A shark's digestive system can break down bone. Large pieces that don't break down may stay in the stomach and get vomited back out.

Yes. Wrong exit. And here's where it gets ridiculous.

Some sharks can push their stomach out through their mouth, get rid of unwanted material, then pull it back inside.

Imagine turning your stomach inside out because lunch was being difficult.

So your bones don't have one guaranteed destination. Some can break down. Stubborn pieces may get returned to sender.

Congratulations. You weren't just lunch. You were a customer service issue.
