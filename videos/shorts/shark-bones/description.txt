If a shark ate you, would it poop out your bones?

Terrible vacation. Surprisingly weird biology.

Shark digestion can break down bone, while large indigestible material can remain in the stomach and be regurgitated. Some sharks can even turn their stomach out through their mouth and retract it. There is no one guaranteed route for every piece of material.

The person, toy skeleton thought bubble, inside-out sock and beach customer-service desk are comedy. Stomach diagrams are simplified cartoon illustrations, not actual anatomy footage. No attack is depicted and humans are not presented as typical shark prey.
