# Production notes — Without Us

Original English narration: 32 chapters, 6,271 whitespace-separated words excluding headings. Kokoro am_michael, speed 1.07. Native audio 2785.650 seconds, including short chapter pauses. No additional tempo adjustment or music. Final container 2785.667 seconds, rounded 46:26.

46 source contact sheets, **4 columns × 4 rows** each: 736 panels, **695 unique selected illustrations**. Mean 4.008 seconds; 446 simple scenes at 3–4 seconds and 249 explanations at 5–6 seconds. 170 cuts are near caption starts/ends where feasible; no claim of word-level visual synchronization. Extra panels are globally assigned to chapters by thematic eligibility, preserving the primary visual order; final curated shots are authoritative.

Grid boundaries measured across perpendicular panels; 6-pixel inset; aspect-preserving scale and fixed center crop to 1280×720, 24 fps. No zoom, pan, shake, header or counter. Static illustrated film, not full character animation. Contact-sheet source cells are lower resolution than the export; no native-HD/4K art claim. User chose to keep this method after discussing softness.

878 English captions from Kokoro token timing, burned into picture and supplied as matching SRT. DejaVu Sans bold29px, outline2px, shadow1px, side65px and bottom28px margins. No permanent black strip. Full normalized caption text matches narration.

Own-source measured two-pass loudness normalization. Final mono48kHz AAC nominal 64k, -16.83 LUFS integrated, -2.30 dBTP true peak, 2.30 LU range. Settings are specific to this narration. Final AAC is one generation from the original WAV, using own-source measured normalization and the oversampled peak limiter/gain recorded in normalization-settings.json. Earlier rejected AAC files were not used as encoding sources. Video was copied during final audio refinement.

All humans vanish in the premise, with no cause, bodies, later survivors or self-maintaining AI assumed. Riverton, Scout and Juniper are fictional examples. Named pets occur only in the first-days/opening-day story and an explicit ending rewind; later animals are distinct individuals/generations. All scene plans exclude human figures. Thumbnail WITHOUT US represents a much later overgrown city, not the next day.

Five panels were curated: two animal-survival rankings, two airless-Moon meteor illustrations and one mammoth in a future-Earth diagram. The latter three now use source45/cells0–2 with craters, local dust ejecta and living modern species. Source07/cell12 and source34/cell12 were replaced by neutral care requirements and varied animals in source44/cells0–1. No breed is assigned guaranteed survival/failure. Original rejected source panels remain for provenance; do not reuse.

Scientific limits: no global blackout hour, universal dam/tower collapse date, synchronized nuclear destruction, radiation-is-harmless inference, instant preindustrial climate, all-land-becomes-forest claim, extinct-species return or immortal pet. See SOURCES.md, project/claim-checks.json, story-bible.json and curation-notes.json.

Validation: complete decode, streams, duration, chapters, caption bounds/order/full text, unique source panels, continuous frame timing, class pacing, final-audio measurement, ZIP CRC and representative visual review. Not exhaustive human listening or a site-specific engineering forecast. Prepared for owner upload; no YouTube publication or scheduling.
