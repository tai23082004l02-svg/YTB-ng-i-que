TITLE
If Humans Disappeared Tomorrow, What Would Survive?

DESCRIPTION
Tomorrow morning, your alarm will ring. Your phone will light up. Somewhere, a dog will wait beside a door. But nobody will come.

What if every human disappeared at the same moment, while animals, plants, weather, buildings, and machines remained? Follow an original illustrated thought experiment from the first minutes to growing seasons, decades, centuries, and the distant traces of our civilization.

Electric grids, water systems, pets, livestock, industrial hazards, forests, oceans, archives, and objects in space would all have different futures. Nature could recover in some ways while pollution, introduced species, climate change, and irreversible losses continued to matter.

This is not a prediction of a real event or one exact global collapse schedule. Riverton and Scout are fictional examples. Scout appears only during the opening-day/first-days story and an explicit return to that opening—not as a pet surviving centuries. No cause for the disappearance, surviving people, or autonomous caretaker civilization is assumed.
