# Sources and scientific limits — Without Us

Original English illustrated documentary for **The Long Wonder**. Accessed 2026-10-24. Title: **If Humans Disappeared Tomorrow, What Would Survive?** This is a conditional thought experiment, not a forecast of an actual disappearance or a calculated worldwide timetable. No copyrighted papers/PDFs are redistributed.

## Sources consulted

1. **U.S. Energy Information Administration, “Delivery to consumers / The electric power grid.”** https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/electricity/delivery-to-consumers.php — balancing supply and demand, regional interconnections, protection/redundancy and operational organization. No exact hours-to-global-blackout estimate is inferred.
2. **U.S. EPA, Power Resilience Guide for Water and Wastewater Utilities.** https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2016-03/documents/160212-powerresilienceguide508.pdf — downloaded PDF, targeted review of introduction, power-loss consequences, generators and fuel planning. Supports loss of pumping/treatment, pressure-related contamination, sewage discharge/backups and cascading infrastructure dependencies. It concerns managed real emergencies, not a simulation of permanent human absence. No universal water-outage or subway-flooding deadline is claimed.
3. **ASPCA, Disaster Preparedness.** https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/general-pet-care/disaster-preparedness — water, food, medicines, shelter, confinement/evacuation and special animal needs. Applied qualitatively; not a study assigning survival probabilities by breed. Scout and Juniper are fictional examples, not recommendations to leave animals unattended.
4. **U.S. Department of Energy, “Nuclear 101: How Does a Nuclear Reactor Work?”** https://www.energy.gov/ne/articles/nuclear-101-how-does-nuclear-reactor-work — chain reaction, control, fuel, coolant and light-water reactor distinctions. Conceptual illustrations are not detailed engineering designs or operational instructions.
5. **U.S. DOE, “5 Fast Facts about Spent Nuclear Fuel.”** https://www.energy.gov/ne/articles/5-fast-facts-about-spent-nuclear-fuel — solid ceramic fuel, initial pool storage and later suitable dry-cask storage. No operational site count, fuel mass, cooling time or transport-safety percentage is used as a prediction for the thought experiment.
6. **IAEA, Radioactive waste and spent fuel storage.** https://www.iaea.org/topics/storage — storage purpose, safety functions and management needs. **IAEA-hosted infographic, Dry Storage Technologies — The Timeline:** https://www.iaea.org/sites/default/files/22/06/dry_storage_technologies_the_timeline.pdf — downloaded and read for storage categories and historical development; the poster is not a heat-removal standard. Heat production after shutdown, cooling and differences between pools and dry systems must remain distinct. Passive does not mean immune to weather, aging or every future failure. No claim of synchronized reactor accidents, universal containment outcomes or nuclear-weapon detonations.
7. **Paul Gaudette and Deborah Slaton, NPS Preservation Brief 15, Preservation of Historic Concrete.** https://www.nps.gov/orgs/1739/upload/preservation-brief-15-concrete.pdf — downloaded PDF; targeted sections on deterioration, moisture, drainage, freeze-thaw, steel corrosion, carbonation, chlorides, expansion and maintenance. Supports mechanisms, not a fixed lifespan for every building or bridge. Humid/salty and sheltered/dry exposures differ.
8. **NOAA Fisheries, Reopening Rivers for Migratory Fish.** https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/national/habitat-conservation/reopening-rivers-migratory-fish — barriers, connectivity and habitat consequences of restoration/removal. This is evidence about river processes, not proof that all unattended dams fail at a given date or that every breach is beneficial.
9. **T. G. Deryabina et al. (2015), “Long-term census data reveal abundant wildlife populations at Chernobyl.”** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2015.08.017 — bibliographic metadata checked via Crossref; public abstract read through Semantic Scholar, with Europe PMC confirming the matching paper/PMID 26439334. Abstract reports large-mammal abundance and explicitly separates population abundance from potential individual radiation effects. Not claimed as full publisher-paper review. The film does not claim radiation is harmless, every species recovered, or every contaminated site is healthy. Illustrations are generic, not field photographs or actual research data.
10. **USDA APHIS, Feral Swine: Managing an Invasive Species.** https://www.aphis.usda.gov/operational-wildlife-activities/feral-swine — feral population impacts and risks. Existence of free-living populations does not imply every confined domestic animal survives or every feral population benefits native ecosystems.
11. **NOAA Marine Debris Program, Derelict Fishing Gear.** https://marinedebris.noaa.gov/what-marine-debris/derelict-fishing-gear — gear can continue affecting/entangling organisms after active fishing ends. Film deliberately omits location-specific mortality totals and does not make the disappearance of fishing an instant recovery guarantee.
12. **NOAA Marine Debris Program, Plastic.** https://marinedebris.noaa.gov/what-marine-debris/plastic — fragmentation, microplastics, environmental persistence and different materials/conditions. No universal polymer lifetime, intact eternal bottle, or guaranteed million-year preservation claim.
13. **NASA Science, Is it too late to prevent climate change?** https://science.nasa.gov/climate-change/faq/is-it-too-late-to-prevent-climate-change/ — stopping emissions does not instantly restore past temperatures; elevated temperatures and other responses persist. The abrupt disappearance scenario also changes aerosols, land management, fires and short-lived pollutants, so the film does not adopt one precise zero-emissions temperature curve as its forecast.
14. **G. A. Schmidt and A. Frank (2018), The Silurian Hypothesis: Would it be possible to detect an industrial civilization in the geological record?** https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.03748 — public abstract consulted for geological fingerprints, detectability and the need to distinguish industrial from some natural signals. Not evidence that an earlier technological civilization existed. This episode's focus is the legacy of our current civilization, not a new discovery claim.
15. **ESA, Space Debris FAQ.** https://www.esa.int/Space_Safety/Space_Debris/Space_Debris_FAQ_Frequently_asked_questions — atmospheric drag, altitude-dependent persistence, collisions, reentry and physical/functional distinctions. No universal satellite lifetime, claim every satellite falls immediately, or assumption current debris counts remain fixed. Old mitigation guidelines on the page are not presented as a current legal standard.
16. **NASA Science, Moon Facts.** https://science.nasa.gov/moon/facts/ — sparse exosphere and airless-surface context. Absence of Earth-like rain/wind does not make footprints or landers eternally unchanged; impacts and space weathering remain relevant. No exact footprint or probe preservation date is claimed.

17. **World Nuclear Association, Storage and Disposal of Radioactive Waste.** https://world-nuclear.org/information-library/nuclear-fuel-cycle/nuclear-waste/storage-and-disposal-of-radioactive-waste — industry-association technical overview, not regulator guidance. Storage sections consulted for heat generation, pool-to-dry distinctions and examples of air-convection heat removal in suitable dry systems. No manufacturer capacities, safety guarantees, cost, lifespan, cooling-time or inventory estimates are adopted as predictions for unattended sites.

## Rules and reasoning specific to this film

- **All human beings disappear at the same moment; no cause is specified.** Animals, weather, infrastructure and existing contents initially remain. No bodies, later survivors, rescue crews, hidden robot civilization or self-maintaining AI archive are assumed.
- Riverton is a **fictional composite town**. The red-brick cafe/ochre awning provides a recognizable site, not a precise forecast of a real city.
- Scout (tan dog, white chest, blue collar) and Juniper (black/white cat, green eyes) belong only to opening-day/first-days examples. The ending explicitly rewinds to the opening. Later feral animals are distinct individuals or generations. No immortal pet.
- Scout's yard gate was **already open** in the chosen example; no unexplained rescue or magically opening barrier. His eventual survival is left unknown. Access, confinement, health, traits, temperature, water and food create different outcomes; no breed gets a universal pass/fail verdict.
- Time windows organize mechanisms, not empirical dates from one integrated model. Systems continue or fail differently according to design, maintenance state, weather, geography, stored inputs and cascading dependencies.
- Forestry/succession/fire passages use general ecological reasoning: establishment needs suitable substrate, water and propagules; disturbances have differing effects. No instant mature forest, universal climax state or assertion all cities burn.
- Structural decay, individual component failure, loss of a building's function and disappearance of its traces are different endpoints. No exact global collapse year.
- Wildlife abundance ≠ individual health ≠ complete biodiversity recovery. Stopping human disturbance does not undo extinctions or biological introductions. Some conservation-dependent species can lose support.
- Species recovery, pollution, climate persistence and cultural/physical traces can coexist; human absence is not offered as a policy prescription or an automatic ecological paradise.
- Digital media require readers, formats and power as well as physical storage. Spacecraft trajectory, working transmitter, physical artifact and an understood message are different kinds of persistence.
- All future images are **original conceptual illustrations**, not actual footage of an event, engineering certifications, measured plots or site-specific reconstructions. The thumbnail depicts a long hypothetical interval after disappearance, not the next morning.

## Access scope

Primary public guidance and the readable material above were checked for the mechanisms used. Some guessed landing-page paths returned errors; only successfully retrieved content is treated as read. NRC pages were inaccessible in this session; reactor/storage discussion uses the accessible DOE and IAEA material, not a claimed NRC review. Publisher access to the wildlife paper was unavailable; only the verified public abstract and metadata were consulted. Research downloads and caches stay outside the repository; this file supplies references and our original interpretation.
