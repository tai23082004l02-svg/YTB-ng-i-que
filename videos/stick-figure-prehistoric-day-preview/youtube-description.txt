TITLE
A Day in the Stone Age — Stick Figure Preview

DESCRIPTION
Your alarm clock is the cold. What happens next?

Follow an imagined hunter-gatherer day in Europe about 20,000 years ago: keeping a fire alive, making tools, finding food, returning from an uncertain hunt, and sharing work at camp.

This short style preview uses expressive stick figures, American English narration and English subtitles. The characters and daily story are fictional; the background draws on archaeological evidence for tools, clothing, food gathering and social life. There was no single way to live in prehistory.

Sources: Smithsonian Human Origins — Stone Tools, Making Clothing, Hearths & Shelters, Social Life; Natural History Museum Vienna — Ice Age Children. Full links and reconstruction limits are in SOURCES.md.

Original illustrations and script. Synthetic narration: Kokoro am_michael. Static illustrated scenes with cuts; no independent character animation. Prepared for owner upload.
