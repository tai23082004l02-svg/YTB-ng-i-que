# Sources and reconstruction limits

This original preview imagines one summer day for a small Homo sapiens hunter-gatherer group in Europe approximately 20,000 years ago. The camp, people, route, division of tasks, unsuccessful hunt, meal and exact sequence of activities are fictional. It is not a reconstruction of one excavated settlement or a universal prehistoric daily schedule.

- [Smithsonian: Stone Tools](https://humanorigins.si.edu/evidence/behavior/stone-tools) — skilled manufacture of flakes, hafted points and scrapers; use of stone and organic materials. Broad technological background, not a precise date for every illustrated object.
- [Smithsonian: Making Clothing](https://humanorigins.si.edu/evidence/behavior/making-clothing) — hide-working tools and bone/ivory needles used for sewn garments. Clothing cuts, colors and shoulder patches here are artistic character identifiers, not recovered fashions.
- [Smithsonian: Hearths & Shelters](https://humanorigins.si.edu/evidence/behavior/hearths-shelters) — fire, cooking and gathering at hearths. The specific ember-keeping routine is a plausible fictional scene, not direct evidence of an exact morning ritual.
- [Smithsonian: Social Life](https://humanorigins.si.edu/human-characteristics/social-life) — food sharing, care and social networks. Exact conversations, family relationships and task assignments cannot be recovered from these images or this script.
- [Natural History Museum Vienna: Hunting mammoth, collecting berries](https://www.nhm.at/en/exhibitions/permanent_exhibitions/ice_age_children/mammoth_berries) — diverse animal and plant resources used by Ice-Age people. Diet depended on geography and season. No named plant species, guaranteed food yield or precise daily nutritional claim is made.
- [Natural History Museum Vienna: A life of travelling](https://www.nhm.at/en/exhibitions/permanent_exhibitions/ice_age_children/life_of_travelling) — movement in relation to resources and protective fur/leather clothing.

The background is a stylized open summer landscape, not a surveyed regional vegetation model. Reindeer are illustrative quarry; the film does not assign a hunting success rate. Plant recognition is learned knowledge, and the film is not a foraging guide. People are represented as stick figures for this style test. No dinosaurs or modern technologies are shown. Speech and jokes are modern narration, not quotations attributed to prehistoric people.
