TITLE
Before the Alarm Clock: A Day in the Life of Hunter-Gatherers

DESCRIPTION
What filled a day before farms, cities, and alarm clocks?

Follow an illustrated day of gathering food, making tools, raising children, sharing knowledge, and spending time together. This original animated explainer explores the skills and relationships that helped people navigate a changing world.

There was no single prehistoric routine. This video presents a composite, illustrative day, not a reconstruction of one specific community. Evidence from archaeology and comparisons with more recent foraging societies have limits.
