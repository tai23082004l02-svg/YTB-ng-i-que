TITLE
A Starship Leaves Earth. Nobody On Board Will See Its Destination.

DESCRIPTION
What if your home was a starship—and the people who chose its destination died centuries before you were born?

Follow Lyra, a child leaving Earth, and Sera, a different descendant living centuries later, through an original fictional generation-ship story. Between ordinary meals, repairs, and decisions, we examine the real science and the unsolved problems behind a journey to another star.

Propulsion is only the beginning. Wayfarer also needs shielding, artificial gravity, food, water, medicine, manufacturing, preserved knowledge, and a society that can keep learning. Who gets to decide what the journey is for when the founders are gone?

This is a thought experiment, not a launched mission, a certified ship design, or an announcement of a safe second Earth. Proposed technologies and population models are distinguished from demonstrated capabilities. No fixed crew number or survival probability is presented as a guarantee.
