# Sources and interpretation notes — Born Between Stars

This is an **original fictional generation-ship story with scientific explanations**, not a report of a launched mission, a completed engineering design, a medical safety finding, or an announced habitable destination. The thought experiment follows Lyra, who leaves Earth as a child, and Sera, a different descendant living centuries later. The founders do not remain alive throughout the journey.

Sources were consulted during production on 24 October 2026. A proposal or a historical experiment is not presented as a demonstrated capability for a centuries-long human voyage.

## Distance, speed, energy and the destination

[NASA Science — Exoplanets](https://science.nasa.gov/exoplanets/) supplies the nearby-world context and describes Proxima Centauri b as about four light-years away. Proxima is used to explain the scale of the nearest stars; **Wayfarer's candidate habitat is fictional**, not a claim that Proxima b or another nearby world has been confirmed as a safe second Earth.

The numerical examples are elementary illustrative calculations, saved in `project/physics-checks.json`:

- Exact SI speed of light: 299,792,458 m/s.
- One percent of that speed is about three million m/s.
- A constant-speed crossing of 4.5 light-years at 0.01 c takes 450 years, before acceleration, braking or other trajectory requirements.
- Classical kinetic energy per kilogram at that speed is about 4.49 trillion joules; relativistic correction at this speed is small. This is **not** total propulsion energy, fuel mass, engine performance or a trajectory design.
- Time dilation at 0.01 c does not convert centuries into an ordinary short human voyage.

The spacecraft's mass, engine, fuel, thermal design, shielding and guidance have not been integrated or certified. Braking is not reduced to a universal “twice the fuel” rule. Fast flyby, rendezvous and a usable long-term arrival are different problems. Star maps, route arrows, collector-like hardware, ring sizes and planetary views are conceptual and not to scale. No graph in the film is original observational or engineering-test data.

## Air, water, food and regenerative life support

- [NASA — Environmental Control and Life Support Systems](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/environmental-control-and-life-support-systems-eclss/): atmosphere control, ventilation, water recovery, air revitalization and oxygen generation are interacting hardware systems requiring operations and maintenance.
- [NASA — Water recovery milestone on the International Space Station](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/station/iss-research/nasa-achieves-water-recovery-milestone-on-international-space-station/): reports a demonstrated 98% water-recovery goal with particular hardware, including the brine processor. It does not establish indefinite closure of all material loops or a self-sustaining interstellar biosphere. The general ECLSS overview and this milestone describe different scopes/configurations; the narration avoids turning one percentage into a universal performance claim.
- [ESA MELiSSA — Closed Loop Concept](https://www.esa.int/Enabling_Support/Space_Engineering_Technology/Melissa/Closed_Loop_Concept): recovery of food, water and oxygen through interacting biological and physical processes is a research objective.
- [ESA MELiSSA — Closed Loop Compartments](https://www.esa.int/Enabling_Support/Space_Engineering_Technology/Melissa/Closed_Loop_Compartments): compartments perform different processing functions; material pathways, microorganisms, photosynthesis and higher plants have distinct roles. Ongoing work and incomplete conversion are not depicted as a solved miniature Earth.

A garden is not a complete life-support system. Food needs more than calories; reserves and separated systems can matter. A backup sharing the same vulnerable utility can suffer a common failure. Small continuing losses, inaccessible residues and degradation can matter over long durations. The film does not assign a particular leak rate or claim that no recycling system could ever improve on present hardware.

Microbes are not universally harmful. Beneficial processes and contamination require context, sampling, instrumentation and interpretation. No laboratory image identifies a specific pathogen or provides a biological manufacturing protocol.

## Radiation, health and artificial gravity

- [NASA Human Research Program — Space Radiation](https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazards/space-radiation/): space radiation risks require measurement and risk assessment; Earth and deep-space conditions differ. No wall thickness or material is declared a universally safe solution. Particle interactions and secondary radiation make shielding a design problem rather than a magic barrier.
- [NASA Human Research Program — Hazards](https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazards/): human spaceflight involves multiple interacting risks; short missions and existing analogues do not demonstrate centuries of community health.
- [NASA — Gravity Fields](https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazard-gravity-fields/): changes in loading affect health and adaptation; existing human spaceflight does not establish multigenerational artificial-gravity safety.
- [NASA — Isolation and Confinement](https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazard-isolation-and-confinement/): selection, support, teamwork, communication delays and analogue research inform the discussion. These are not a tested centuries-long political or psychological model.
- Angie Bukley, William Paloski and Gilles Clément, **Physics of Artificial Gravity**, [NASA NTRS document 20070001008](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20070001008). Record, abstract and targeted full text consulted, including rotation mechanics, radius/rotation tradeoffs, gravity gradients and Coriolis effects. This is background on concepts and human factors, not validation of Wayfarer or of reproduction across generations.

A rotating floor can provide a gravity-like experience through mechanics. Apparent down in the rotating ring points toward the **outer rim**, not toward a planet-like mass at its center. Familiar weight sensations do not prove normal lifetime health, pregnancy, development or multigenerational reproduction in that environment. Ball-path sketches are qualitative illustrations, not calculated experiments or exact rotating-frame trajectories.

The ship's clinic is a fictional example of remote care needs. Preventive care, aging, disability access, mental health, medicines, equipment and training are included without presenting the film as medical guidance or claiming an existing self-sufficient interstellar hospital. Interior daylight colors are illustrative habitat lighting, not evidence of an atmosphere outside the vessel. Memorial lights are symbolic; no spacecraft open-flame policy is asserted.

## Population models and ethical limits

F. Marin and C. Beluffi, **Computing the minimal crew for a multi-generational space travel towards Proxima Centauri b** (2018), [open abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.03856). Abstract consulted. The model's outcomes depend on explicit assumptions, including demographic parameters and reproductive restrictions. Its stated success criterion, long voyage and Monte Carlo scenarios are not a universal minimum passenger number or a guarantee for a real society.

The film intentionally gives **no magical minimum crew count**. Genetic, demographic, skills, resource and institutional questions are not interchangeable. Stored reproductive material does not replace every function of a living population. A model's restrictions are not endorsed as coercive policy.

The passages about consent, privacy, institutions, dissent, descendants and the absence of an easy exit are **ethical questions and original reasoning**, not findings attributed to a space agency or a claim that science mandates one government. Descendants are treated as participants with their own lives, not replacements owned by the founding plan.

## Alternatives and the limits of proposals

[Breakthrough Initiatives — Starshot](https://breakthroughinitiatives.org/initiative/3) describes a research concept for very small light-driven probes. Its proof-of-concept ambitions and stated future goals are not evidence of an operating transport system or a crew-rated light-sail service.

Robotic probes avoid many human-habitat requirements but still need propulsion, survival, power and communications. A flyby does not automatically certify a landing site. Century-scale healthy human hibernation is not a demonstrated mission capability. Embryo/reproductive-material concepts shift problems toward care, development, medical uncertainty and ethics; the film does not claim that autonomous human upbringing or a working embryo colony exists.

## Reliability, chronology and illustration provenance

Maintenance, manufacturing chains, expertise, storage formats and institutional renewal are discussed as practical constraints and hypothetical design questions. There is no claim that every component lasts centuries or that a general fabricator can make every specialized part. The pump incident is an invented example of how reserves, independence, information and trust interact, not a real flight accident.

All visuals are original AI-generated 2D illustrations. `project/story-bible.json` preserves ages, clothing, time jumps, recurring locations and the conceptual twin-ring ship. Lyra's two hair buns/circle patch distinguish her from Sera's single braid/triangle patch. Founder images appearing in Sera's era are archives or separated historical comparisons, not surviving founders.

Contact sheets are **4 columns by 4 rows**, cell numbers 0–15. Actual panel boundaries and fixed crops are in `project/grid-crops.json`; generated rows are not assumed perfectly equal. Selected panels and timing are in `project/shots.json`, and exclusions in `project/curation-notes.json`.

One generated ring diagram, sheet-09/cell-0, used misleading parallel page-down arrows instead of radial outward apparent-down directions. It is excluded from the final video and replaced with a neutral habitat-layout explanation. The excluded source remains for provenance and must not be restored as a valid force diagram.

Two initially guessed NASA hazard subpages returned 404 and were not used as evidence. Research PDFs, downloaded article HTML, model cache and intermediate WAV files remain local; full source articles are not republished in the repository.
