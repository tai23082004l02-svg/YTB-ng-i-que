# Production notes — Born Between Stars

Original English narration: 32 chapters, 5,798 whitespace-separated words excluding headings. Kokoro am_michael, speed 1.07. Native audio 2378.45 seconds with short chapter pauses. No additional tempo adjustment and no music. Final container 2378.458 seconds, rounded 39:38.

40 original contact sheets, **4 columns × 4 rows** each: 640 source panels, **594 unique selected illustrations**. This is the first completed film using the updated faster pacing: average 4.004 seconds, 417 simple scenes at 3–4 seconds and 177 explanatory scenes at 5–6 seconds. 179 cuts are near caption start/end boundaries where the duration constraints allow; remaining cuts use the ordered visual plan and feasible fixed-frame timing. No claim of word-level visual alignment.

Actual row/column transitions are detected across all four perpendicular cells and saved in project/grid-crops.json, with a 6-pixel source inset to remove separators. Scaling preserves aspect ratio and a fixed center crop fills 1280 × 720 at 24 fps. No pan, zoom, shake, top header or scene counter. This is static illustrated video, not full character animation. Panels are scaled from contact-sheet images; this is not native 4K art.

767 English caption cues from Kokoro token timing, burned into the picture in DejaVu Sans bold, 29 px at 1280 × 720, 2 px outline, 1 px shadow, 65 px side margins and 28 px bottom margin. No permanent black strip; matching SRT supplied.

Measured two-pass audio normalization from this narration. Final mono 48 kHz AAC nominal 128k, measured -16.74 LUFS integrated and -2.17 dBTP true peak; 2.40 LU loudness range. Do not apply these measured offsets to a different voice track.

Wayfarer, people, incidents and destination are fictional. Physics examples do not establish an integrated mission design. The ship has no certified propulsion, ecosystem, medical, shielding or population solution. Lyra ages from child to adult to elder; Sera is a distinct descendant centuries later. Earth pictures in the later era are archives. Governance passages are ethical questions and original reasoning.

Visual curation: sheet-09/cell-0 used parallel page-down vectors instead of outward radial apparent-down directions in a rotating ring. Shot 160 instead uses sheet-32/cell-4, a neutral habitat layout. The excluded panel remains in the source for provenance and must not be reused as valid physics.

Thumbnail was generated and edited with image generation, then formatted as a 1280 × 720 JPEG. BORN BETWEEN STARS features child Sera, stars outside and a clearly framed archival Earth photograph inside the cabin.

Validation includes full-file decode, streams, chapter count, caption order/bounds, continuous frame timing, unique panels, pacing by scene class, audio measurements, ZIP CRC and representative visual review.
